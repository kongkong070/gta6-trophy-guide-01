Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough — about 200 hours to the platinum, per PowerPyx. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so keep a manual save at the start of each chapter. Cheats switch trophies off, so leave them off for the whole run. Sources: PowerPyx trophy guide & roadmap; Push Square trophy guide.

    Only one trophy is possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate with Lucia on the balcony of Jason’s house in Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth. Every other trophy can still be earned in free roam after the story. Source: PowerPyx; also flagged by GTABase via CryptoGames.

    No online trophies: all 55 are single-player and offline. Sources: PowerPyx; GTA Six Insider.

      How this guide was built & its sources

Every guide below them is written in our own words for this entry; the facts it rests on are cited beside each trophy. Where launch-era guides disagree or a method is still unconfirmed, the guide says so instead of guessing — see Bad Luck, I Guess and Ten Pin.
