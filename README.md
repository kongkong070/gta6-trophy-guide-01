An offline guide and tracker for all 55 Grand Theft Auto VI trophies on PlayStation 5. Every trophy has a guide written in our own words, with steps and named sources, plus a five-stage roadmap (~200 hours) from first story mission to the platinum. The built-in tracker keeps your checklist in localStorage, with export/import, filters by category, and a spoiler setting — no account, no network, nothing leaves your browser.

## Run

Open `app/index.html` in any browser — the app is fully offline.
